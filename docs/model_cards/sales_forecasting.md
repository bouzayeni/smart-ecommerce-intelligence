# Model card — Daily GMV forecasting

> Generated by `ml/train_forecasting.py` on 2026-11-05 from MLflow run `f076b097b6304a75867139863c7cd9fa`. Re-run to refresh.

## Task

- **Prediction:** daily GMV of revenue orders, 1–90 days ahead. Evaluated over
  the first 30, 60, 90 days after the origin.
- **Series:** `marts.agg_daily_sales`, 2017-01-01 → 2018-08-21 (598 days).
- **Historical data.** The "future" forecast covers the 90 days after the extract
  ends, so no actual values exist for it.

## Evaluation design (no shuffling)

- **Rolling-origin backtest.** Origins 2017-10-01, 2017-12-01, 2018-02-01. Each fold
  trains on all days up to and including the origin and forecasts the next
  90 days. Model selection uses the mean backtest WAPE at 90 days.
- **Test.** Origin 2018-05-23: the last 90 days of the window, used once.
- **Leakage controls.** Scalers (LSTM) and model orders (SARIMA, chosen by AIC) are fitted on
  training data only. XGBoost uses lags of 7 days or more and feeds back its own predictions.
  Calendar regressors (holidays, Black Friday) are known in advance.
- **Prediction intervals** at 80%. SARIMA and Prophet use their
  native intervals. Seasonal naive, XGBoost and LSTM use conformal intervals: the
  80% quantile of absolute backtest errors per horizon bucket
  (1–30, 31–60, 61–90 days).
- **MAPE** is computed only on days with actual GMV ≥ R$ 1,000.
  **WAPE** (Σ|error| ÷ Σ actual) is the headline metric.

**Verdict:** selected **prophet** (lowest mean backtest WAPE at 90 days) has test WAPE 32.2% at 90 days vs 28.6% for the seasonal naive baseline; the best model on test is **lstm** (17.3%). The 80% interval of the selected model covered 33% of test days.

**The selection did not generalise.** The backtest winner is worse than the seasonal naive baseline on test. With only 3 backtest origins the model ranking is unstable: the backtest and test rankings disagree. The selection is kept as specified and not replaced by the test winner, because choosing on the test set would make its error an in-sample number. All models' forecasts are published (`ml.sales_forecast`, `is_selected` flag).

SARIMA order chosen on the final full-series fit: (1, 1, 2).

## Limitations

- **Short history.** About 20 months with a single Black Friday. Yearly seasonality is not
  modelled (Prophet `yearly_seasonality: false`). A Black Friday effect learned from one
  occurrence is uncertain.
- **Truncated extract.** The series ends 2018-08-21, before the extract's truncation
  (BR-02). Forecasts past that date cannot be checked.
- **Small LSTM training set.** Only a few hundred overlapping training windows exist, so it is
  included for comparison, not because deep learning suits a series this short.
