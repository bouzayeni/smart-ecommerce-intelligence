## 1. The marketplace grew strongly, then flattened in 2018

- GMV for months 01–07: R$ 2,913,089 in 2017 vs R$ 7,596,164 in 2018 (+161%).
- Peak complete month: 2017-11 with R$ 1,172,192 and 7,421 orders.
- Month-on-month growth in 2018: 2018-01 +27.9%, 2018-02 -11.1%, 2018-03 +17.7%, 2018-04 +0.3%, 2018-05 -0.9%, 2018-06 -10.9%, 2018-07 +1.9%.

**Hypothesis:** Year-on-year growth came mostly from 2017's ramp-up; a forecast should not extrapolate the 2017 slope into late 2018 (see the forecasting model card).

## 2. Almost every customer buys once

- 94,046 customers placed 97,222 revenue orders (1.034 orders per customer).
- Repeat purchase rate: 3.03% (2,852 customers with two or more orders).
- Customers active again in the month after their first order, all cohorts pooled: 0.48%.

**Hypothesis:** Growth depends on acquisition, not loyalty. This is also why the repurchase and recommendation models have little signal to learn from (see their model cards).

## 3. Spend is concentrated in the top decile

- The top 10% of customers by spend account for 38.3% of GMV (each spent at least R$ 319).
- The bottom half of customers accounts for 19.2%.
- 90.6% of the top-decile customers placed a single order (`ml.customer_rfm`, same customers).

**Hypothesis:** The top decile is mostly people who bought one expensive basket, not loyal heavy buyers, so price mix matters more than purchase frequency.

## 4. Sales are concentrated in the South-East, and delivery quality varies by state

- Top three states by GMV: SP 37.2%, RJ 13.4%, MG 11.7% (together 62.4%).
- Late-delivery rate, states with at least 500 eligible deliveries: highest MA 18.2% (average 22.1 days), lowest SP 4.1% (9.0 days); overall 6.9%.
- 59.7% of sellers are based in SP.

**Hypothesis:** Distance from the sellers, most of whom are in SP, is the likely driver; the data has no carrier or route information to confirm it.

## 5. Late deliveries are strongly associated with bad reviews

- Average review score: 4.28 for on-time orders vs 2.21 for late orders (lower by 2.07 points).
- 1-star reviews: 6.7% of on-time orders vs 55.3% of late orders.
- Overall average score 4.09; on-time rate 93.1%.

**Hypothesis:** Delivery performance and delivery-date estimates in the slowest states are the most promising lever on satisfaction to test. This is a correlation; an experiment would be needed to measure the effect.

## 6. No single category dominates; most money is paid by card in instalments

- Top five categories by item GMV: health_beauty 9.1%, watches_gifts 8.3%, bed_bath_table 7.9%, sports_leisure 7.3%, computers_accessories 6.7% (together 39.3%).
- Payment value by type: Credit card 78.4%, Boleto bancário 18.0%, Voucher 2.2%, Debit card 1.3%; credit card payments average 3.5 instalments.
- Freight is 14.2% of GMV (average R$ 23 per order).

**Hypothesis:** A broad catalogue spreads risk; instalments suggest price sensitivity, so free-instalment promotions are a plausible lever to test.

## What the models add

Each model card compares the model with a simple baseline on held-out data; the summary is in [portfolio.md](portfolio.md#results-against-baselines). Finding 2 is the main constraint: with about 3% repeat customers, customer-level models have little history to learn from.
